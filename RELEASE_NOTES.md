MilitaryInfo 0.5.0 - Army Ranks

Added
- 28 Army rank entries: enlisted (Private to Sergeant Major of the Army), warrant officers (WO1 to CW5), officers (2LT to COL), general officers (BG to GEN)
- Each enlisted rank lists sew-on (ASU male and female, AGSU), polished pin-on, and subdued pin-on finishes
- Service stripes and Overseas service bars: sizes, colors, and sleeve placement
- Source paragraph on every entry (DA Pam 670-1 chapter 21)

Known limits
- No rank pictures yet
- Only current insignia. Earlier eras, and other branches, are not loaded
- The 3-year stripe rule and 6-month bar rule come from AR 670-1 text quoted on an unofficial site. The DA Pam text read did not state them
