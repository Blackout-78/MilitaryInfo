MilitaryInfo 0.8.0 - Navy Ranks Begin

Added
- Navy officer and flag officer entries (Ensign to Fleet Admiral) with sleeve stripe pictures drawn to the widths in the Navy Uniform Regulations
- Navy warrant officers (W-1 to W-5), E-1 to E-3 rate marks, the Chief Petty Officer rating badge, and Navy service stripes
- Source section number on every Navy entry (mynavyhr.navy.mil, NAVPERS 15665)

Known limits
- Navy enlisted E-4 to E-9 chevron counts are not loaded: the official page text did not state them
- Officer pictures show stripes only. Star and corps devices are not drawn
- Warrant officer entries have no picture
- Navy uniforms, medals, badges, and earlier eras are not loaded yet
